Our Story

Caked Creations started long before it had a name; back when 19-year-old Aria Rao was just a kid growing up in the suburbs of New Jersey, spending her afternoons in the family kitchen. What began as simple chocolate chip cookies evolved into elaborate birthday cakes and elaborate dessert displays.

As she got older, baking became more than a fun hobby. It became her creative outlet - a space where she could unwind, try new ideas, and make people smile. Eventually, she realized she wanted to turn her passion into something more.

What started in her home kitchen soon grew into a small but mighty business. Aria began taking custom orders, learning how to brand her treats, talk to customers, and manage the behind-the-scenes operations of a growing business.

Now a Business student at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, Aria continues to use what she's learned from Caked Creations to discover new opportunities for entrepreneurship while balancing her academic pursuits.

Through this journey, Aria discovered that her passion for baking connects naturally with her interest in business strategy and innovation. Caked Creations didn't just shape her college experience—it shaped who she is today.
