Danielle Leigh Johnson

I am currently a student at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill studying Media and Journalism, with a minor in Political Science, Philosophy, and Economics (PPE). I'm interested in storytelling and communication, especially how media influences the way people understand and connect with the world around them. I grew up in a large family and have always been surrounded by close friends, which has shaped how much I value relationships and shared experiences. Because of that, the most important part of my life is the people I spend it with, and I try to build a lifestyle centered around connection, laughter, and being present with others. Outside of school, I try to stay active and grounded. Going to the gym is one of my most consistent habits, and I'm always open to trying new activities and experiences when I can. I also enjoy thrifting and cooking in my free time, though I don't always get as much time for them as I'd like. A lot of my time is spent with friends and family, and I also work as a waitress, which keeps me busy and has taught me a lot about staying adaptable when things don't go my way. I joined Zeta Tau Alpha at UNC because I've always been drawn to service and community involvement. Being part of service work is important to me because it gives me a chance to step outside of myself and stay connected to something bigger than my own routine. Overall, I try to keep a balance between staying busy and taking time to slow down, and I value experiences that bring people together in simple, meaningful ways.
